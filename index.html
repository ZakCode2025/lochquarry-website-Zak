Welcome to Lochquarry Outdoor Centre the perfect place for adventure, fun, and unforgettable memories!
Whether you’re 6 or 18, there’s something exciting waiting for you here. From climbing high ropes and exploring the outdoors to splashing through water activities and taking on exciting land challenges, every day at Lochquarry is packed with action and discovery. We welcome schools, families, friends, and youth groups to come and enjoy the amazing beauty of Scottish outdoors together. Our centre has recently added even more awesome activities, giving everyone the chance to try something new, build confidence, make friends, and have loads of fun along the way.
Our activities are divided into three exciting adventure zones:
Land-based activities explore, climb, and challenge yourself on solid ground
Water-based activities dive into thrilling fun on the water
Rope-based activities test your courage high above the ground
No matter your age or experience level, our friendly team is here to help you have a safe, exciting, and memorable adventure. Ready to start your next adventure? We can’t wait to welcome you to Lochquarry Outdoor Centre!.
